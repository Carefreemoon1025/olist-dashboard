Key Insight

Delivery speed is the #1 driver of customer satisfaction. Orders delivered within 10 days average 4.4 stars, while those taking 30+ days drop to 2.2 stars (down 50%). This single factor explains more about customer sentiment than product quality, price, or category. The data points directly to logistics optimization as the highest-ROI lever for improving user experience — especially in remote states with delivery delays exceeding 20%.
